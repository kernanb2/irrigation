Unit D Wiring

Unit D: four irrigation zones (21–24) with a DFRobot SEN0308 moisture sensor each, and the Taidacent ultrasonic sensor that measures the pond level. Same DIN rail kit as Unit E: five blocks of each colour.

Colours mean something different from Unit E.

Red is still +12V, black 5V and green ground. Here grey is the 9V valve rail (Gy1–Gy3 only), yellow is 3.3V for the sensors, and blue carries sensor signals in from the field. Gy4 and Gy5 carry signals too, so they must not be bridged to Gy1–Gy3. Label the grey and yellow blocks with their voltage.

Each block has two clamps. "Inside" faces the ESP32; "outside" takes the field cable. Where two wires share a clamp, crimp them into one twin ferrule. A pill-shaped tag on the diagram (Gn3, Y2, Gy 9V…) means "this wire lands on that block".

Each valve's two wires go straight into its DRV8871's OUT screw terminals; there aren't enough blue blocks for all eight. Fit a 100µF capacitor across VM and GND on each DRV8871, and no diode across the valve (it is pulsed both ways). If a zone opens when you press Close, swap its two valve wires.

Set both LM2596s before anything is connected to them.

Adjust the 9V one to 9.0V and the 5V one to 5.0V with only their inputs wired, then connect the outputs. And as on every unit: unplug the 5V from VIN before plugging in USB to flash.

Build and test, one stage at a time

  1. Rail and 12V. Fit the bridges: red R1–R2, grey Gy1–Gy3 only, yellow Y1–Y4, and all five green. Supply + → F1 → R1, supply − → Gn1. Check for 12V from R1 to green.
  2. 9V rail. Wire the 9V LM2596 to R1 and Gn1, set it to 9.0V, then connect its output to Gy1. Check 9.0V on Gy1–Gy3 and 0V on Gy4 and Gy5.
  3. 5V. Wire the 5V LM2596 to R2 and Gn2, set it to 5.0V, then connect its output to Bk1.
  4. ESP32. Flash it over USB first (Unit D firmware, SECRET_UNIT_ID 4) with nothing else connected. Then Bk1 → VIN, GND → Gn2: Unit D comes online on the dashboard. Wire 3V3 → Y1 and check 3.3V on Y1–Y4.
  5. Ultrasonic sensor. Red → Y1, black → Gn4, yellow → Gy4 (GPIO 27), white → Bu5 (GPIO 26). The Pond Level card should show "Water is X in below the sensor". Hold a board at a measured distance under it to check.
  6. Moisture sensors, one at a time. V → Y2/Y3, G → Gn5, S → its blue block. Its zone card shows a moisture %: high in air, low in a glass of water. Note both readings for calibration.
  7. Valves, one zone at a time. DRV8871 VM, GND, IN1/IN2 and the 100µF cap, then the valve. Press Open, then Close on the dashboard and listen for the click each way.
  8. DS18B20 (optional). VCC → Y4, GND → Gn4, data → Gy5, with the 4.7k from Gy5 to Y4.
